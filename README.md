# Life Quest

A pixel-art life tracker with a virtual pet. Tell your pet what you did today
and it levels up. It cheers when you hit your goals and gets sad (but stays
encouraging) when you slip.


## Features
- Up to 6 goals, each with an attribute (like Fitness) and a weekly target
- Radar chart showing your total points and this week's progress
- Pet that blinks, hops, and reacts to your wins and slips
- Bonus level-up when every goal hits its weekly target
- Progress panel with weekly completion and a 14-day chart

## How to use
1. Add a goal, e.g. "Fitness" / "Go to the gym".
2. Type what you did ("I went to the gym") or tap the ✓ button.
3. Watch your pet and your radar chart grow.

## Run it yourself
Download `index.html` and open it in your browser. Your progress is saved in
your browser, so no account or server is needed.
